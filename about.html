About Me

I'm a software engineer and have been working in the media space since the beginning of my career. I started at a startup, where I worked on social media analytics, intelligent brand discovery and categorization, and a box office prediction system. After that, I moved to a large tech company to work on media sharing in a cloud storage product. In my current role, I started with root of trust firmware attestations and secure boot for multi root of trust systems, before moving to the browser content protection team. Now, I focus on the browser's Content Decryption Module (CDM) for premium video and audio, working to help content providers reach their audience seamlessly and ensure a smooth, uninterrupted experience for our users.
